# East Sussex Roofing: Working Policy Review Register

# MANAGEMENT REVIEW REQUIRED BEFORE PUBLICATION

These are working drafts for site implementation, not legal advice. Management must review, correct, and approve them. A qualified privacy/legal reviewer should confirm the final versions where required.

## Privacy notice: draft scope

Explain what information is collected through quote/contact forms, why it is used, lawful basis where applicable, retention, who receives it, hosting/form providers, customer rights, complaints route, and how to contact the business. Do not claim a provider, retention period, lawful basis, or rights process until management confirms it.

Required placeholders:

- Controller name and registered/contact address: `MANAGEMENT TO CONFIRM`
- Privacy contact email: `MANAGEMENT TO CONFIRM`
- Form provider and hosting recipients: `MANAGEMENT TO CONFIRM`
- Retention period: `MANAGEMENT TO CONFIRM`
- Data-subject rights process: `MANAGEMENT TO CONFIRM`
- ICO registration/details if applicable: `MANAGEMENT TO CONFIRM`

## Cookie notice: draft scope

Describe strictly necessary cookies and any analytics, marketing, chat, maps, embedded media, or third-party services actually used. Do not state that the site uses no cookies until a clean-browser network/storage check has been completed. Optional non-essential storage should not be enabled before valid consent where required.

Required placeholders:

- Consent platform: `MANAGEMENT TO CONFIRM`
- Cookie inventory and durations: `MANAGEMENT TO CONFIRM`
- Analytics/marketing vendors: `MANAGEMENT TO CONFIRM`
- Consent categories and withdrawal method: `MANAGEMENT TO CONFIRM`

## Website terms: draft scope

Cover acceptable use, intellectual property, external links, availability, limitation wording, governing law/jurisdiction, and a clear statement that website information is general information rather than a survey, diagnosis, guarantee, or quote. Do not add warranty or liability wording without management/legal approval.

Required placeholders:

- Legal business name and entity status: `MANAGEMENT TO CONFIRM`
- Registered address: `MANAGEMENT TO CONFIRM`
- Governing law/jurisdiction: `MANAGEMENT TO CONFIRM`
- Contact route for notices: `MANAGEMENT TO CONFIRM`

## Accessibility statement: draft scope

State the target accessibility standard, known limitations, testing date, contact route for accessibility issues, and planned remediation. Do not claim WCAG conformance until keyboard, focus, contrast, reflow, forms, gallery, and zoom checks have been completed.

Current known implementation requirement: the live site showed 320 px horizontal overflow. The new site should state only measured conformance and should retain a visible route for reporting barriers.

## Quote form wording: draft

Use a short notice beside the form: “We use the information you provide to respond to your roofing enquiry. Please see our Privacy Notice for how we handle personal information.”

Management must confirm the form action, recipient, storage, notification route, spam protection, response expectations, and whether attachments/photos are accepted. Do not promise an emergency response or a response time without approval.

## Email migration wording: draft

Use `quotes@eastsussexroofing.co.uk` and `enquiries@eastsussexroofing.co.uk` prominently only after mailbox creation, inbound MX testing, SPF validation, DKIM alignment, DMARC publication/monitoring, and website-form reply testing. Until then, the site must not imply that the branded addresses are operational.

## Publication gate

Before any policy goes live, management must approve the text, replace every `MANAGEMENT TO CONFIRM` placeholder, verify contact identity, confirm the form/data flow, and record the review date and reviewer.
